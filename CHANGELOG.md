# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

First playable **Vice Leonida / Sharaga Edition** vertical slice.

- A responsive, illustrated world hub with district selection, offline solo play, public lobbies and private room codes.
- Four connected districts: Vice Beach, Neon Strip, fictional Шарага College and Port Leonida. Enterable campus lobby, furnished lecture/café wings, pedestrians and social animation.
- Two locally bundled drivable vehicles, a 3D garage and paint selection. Fixed 60 Hz Rapier simulation, wheel-ray suspension, collision-aware orbit, chase and hood cameras, variable render resolution and measured telemetry.
- Legally sourced and optimized GLBs, skeletal motion clips, HDR environment lighting, original 2K road material, coastal water, time/weather, local ambient and vehicle audio and two synthesized radio stations.
- District GPS, custom waypoints, fast travel, courier contracts, a timed circuit, local funds and sandbox modifiers.
- Keyboard remapping, pointer-lock or drag look, touch stick and actions, mirrored layout presets, scale/opacity/visibility editing, fullscreen controls and safe-area HUD.
- Five manual IndexedDB slots plus autosave, validated JSON import/export and local preferences.
- Same-origin Socket.IO rooms with validated and bounded player/vehicle poses, remote avatar interpolation, synchronized conditions and text chat.
- Pinned asset ingestion, strict TypeScript, production build, simulation tests, desktop/mobile browser smoke suite, versioned source/build archives and explicit Drive/GitHub release handoffs.

This release does not include real-world campus replication, explicit interactions, combat, a full traffic/economy simulation, or GTA feature parity. See PROJECT_MEMORY.md for scope.
